# Research programme

The conceptual paper should end with a falsifiable research programme.

## Study stream 1 — Construct validation

Develop measures for:
- temporal fit
- navigation literacy
- safe-space accessibility
- referral continuity
- persistent re-entry
- functional family-support access
- family readiness

Goal: determine whether these are empirically distinguishable constructs.

## Study stream 2 — Service mapping

Map actual entry nodes across Thailand:
- civil
- health
- Islamic
- Christian
- family/community
- mental health
- violence response
- parenting

Measure availability, eligibility, timing, content, referral linkages, privacy/confidentiality, and re-entry capability.

## Study stream 3 — Rapid versus planned pathway

Compare:

~~~text
rapid core + re-entry
vs
planned modular preparation
vs
usual care / existing provision
~~~

Primary outcomes:
- comprehension
- navigation literacy
- safe-space literacy
- referral knowledge
- acceptability
- burden
- completion

## Study stream 4 — Referral completion

Test whether navigation and warm referral improve:
- referral initiation
- completed contact
- time to appropriate service
- re-entry at later life events

## Study stream 5 — Safe-space pathway

Evaluate:
- private-channel discoverability
- perceived safety
- disclosure behaviour
- appropriate escalation
- confidentiality failures
- unintended harms

Safety research must not use relationship persistence as the primary success outcome.

## Study stream 6 — Faith-based implementation

Co-design with Islamic and Christian institutions.

Test the **core + extension** model:
- what must remain institution-specific?
- what minimum cross-cutting functions can be shared?
- which referrals should remain external?
- how is trust affected?
- how should women's/private access be governed?

## Study stream 7 — Longitudinal outcomes

Only after feasibility and safety are established, examine:
- relationship quality
- mental health
- reproductive-health service use
- fertility-treatment access
- parenting readiness
- family well-being
- violence-related help-seeking

Population fertility should remain a distal policy outcome, not the first effectiveness endpoint.

## Falsification agenda

The theory should be considered weakened if studies show that:

1. fragmentation dimensions do not predict functional access;
2. navigation literacy does not explain later service use;
3. persistent re-entry adds no value beyond one-time education;
4. safe-space access does not alter disclosure/help-seeking where privacy risk exists;
5. trusted-node embedding does not affect engagement;
6. rapid and planned pathways do not differ in burden or usefulness;
7. simpler rival models explain outcomes equally well with fewer constructs.
