# Adjudication after five-lens adversarial review

## Decision

The architecture survives, but one central terminology change is recommended.

## Before

```text
Structural Conditions
→ Effective Possibility
→ Family Capability
→ Choice / Action
→ Family / Child Outcomes
```

## Recommended final-facing form

```text
Structural Resources / Conditions
→ Conversion Conditions
→ Family Capability
→ Choice / Action
→ Outcomes
```

where:

```text
Conversion Conditions
=
Access
+ Time/Burden
+ Safety/Autonomy
+ Culture/Meaning
+ Capacity/Continuity
```

## Why change "Effective Possibility"

The Capability Approach already has a mature vocabulary for:

- resources;
- conversion factors;
- capabilities;
- functionings.

"Effective Possibility" remains useful internally as a plain-language intuition, but it currently does not demonstrate enough incremental theoretical value to justify a separate construct in the final manuscript.

Therefore:

```text
EffectivePossibility
→ internal shorthand only
```

unless later evidence demonstrates a distinct prediction or measurement advantage.

## Add outcome horizons

```text
Proximal
→ Intermediate
→ Distal
```

### Proximal
- orientation
- navigation
- safe/private access
- appropriate referral
- help-seeking
- return-route knowledge

### Intermediate
- relationship processes
- caregiver well-being
- continuity
- parenting/reproductive-health service uptake

### Distal
- family well-being
- nurturing-care conditions
- child outcomes
- fertility

## Premarital scope statement

The empirical object is the **premarital period**.

The paper should not imply that premarital contact is:

- the main explanation of low fertility;
- the best family-policy investment by default;
- a substitute for structural social policy;
- a substitute for specialist care;
- a direct cause of child outcomes.

The claim is narrower:

> Premarital contact may be a useful entry window where people can receive non-deferrable orientation, safe/private access, navigation and a reliable route into later support, subject to structural resources, conversion conditions, service capacity, autonomy and safety.

## Five mandatory revision rules

1. **Fertility stays distal.**
2. **All policy recommendations require an alternative-allocation comparator.**
3. **Cultural embeddedness must earn trust/safety empirically.**
4. **Proximal, intermediate and distal outcomes must never be collapsed.**
5. **No internal analytic label becomes a public construct without incremental value.**

## Final kill criterion

If the remaining premarital-specific relations make no distinct prediction, no distinct measurement distinction and no distinct design decision beyond existing capability, access, life-course and implementation frameworks, the paper should be presented as an **integrative conceptual synthesis/application**, not as a new theory.
