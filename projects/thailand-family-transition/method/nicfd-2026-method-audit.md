# NICFD 2026 method audit — premarital gateway paper

## Route and status

This audit was created after reading the current routing repository `morrocwi/main.hub`.

For a paper/review/release task, main.hub routes the work to **glosa** as the method authority. The project-specific evidence workspace is **evidence-forge**. No new mathematical equation or theorem is introduced in this revision, so Toledo was not used as a source of substantive social-science claims.

## What the current project actually has

The evidence-forge project documents the following workflow:

1. structured search families by mechanism/domain;
2. integrative/problem-oriented synthesis;
3. evidence notes and claim-level mapping;
4. backward and forward citation chaining for high-value sources;
5. claim-level citation audit;
6. counterevidence and boundary audit;
7. theory/evidence/falsifier matrices and explicit kill criteria.

The project also contains dedicated evidence attacks for:
- premarital contact and later help-seeking;
- minimum non-deferrable functions under low time feasibility;
- shared couple + private individual access;
- return/re-entry;
- edge/node/capacity diagnosis.

## What is NOT available

The repository does **not** currently preserve a frozen database-by-database search log with exact historical query strings and hit counts for every search family from the beginning of the project.

Therefore the manuscript must NOT claim:
- systematic review;
- PRISMA compliance;
- exhaustive coverage;
- “no evidence exists” without a search-bounded qualifier.

The manuscript may accurately describe the work as:
> integrative, theory-oriented synthesis with a structured evidence workflow, citation chaining, claim-level audit, and explicit counterevidence/boundary checks.

## Claim-strength decisions carried into v4

- Premarital contact → later relationship help-seeking: RETAIN / NARROW; association exists, causality and wider service domains remain open.
- Minimum short package: OPEN; use “candidate non-deferrable functions,” not “minimum sufficient package.”
- Couple-visible + private individual route: RETAIN / NARROW as a safeguarding design principle; private access alone is not a safe system.
- Re-entry: OPEN / PROMISING; separate user-initiated return, transition-triggered offer, and periodic checkup.
- Integration: RETAIN / NARROW; receiving-system availability, connectivity, capacity, quality, and governance must be checked.
- Child outcomes: distal; premarital intervention is not a direct child-development intervention.
- Fertility: distal population outcome; not the first or sole endpoint.
