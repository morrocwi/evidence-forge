# Peer Review Round 5 — Mahidol submission-readiness attack

## Status

This is an adversarial review of `nicfd-2026-full-paper-th-v4-mahidol-hardening.md`.

It is **not** an independent human review and must not be reported as one. The purpose is to test whether the v4 manuscript is ready to be compressed into the NICFD 2026 submission format without reopening the conceptual architecture.

## Overall decision

**Minor-to-moderate revision for content; major revision for conference-format economy.**

The conceptual architecture now survives the earlier attacks. The remaining risk is no longer mainly “is the idea defensible?” but “can the paper show the idea quickly enough, within the conference form, without allowing the method or causal claims to become stronger than the evidence?”

## Reviewer A — conceptual contribution

### Survives

The contribution is now visible:

> premarital is reorganised from a content-delivery moment into a service interface that can orient, provide a private route, navigate, refer, and preserve a route back.

This no longer depends on claiming invention of access theory, safeguarding, preconception care, relationship education, or network governance.

### Remaining correction

Avoid wording that says community/intermediary organisations **can** implement the architecture as if implementation feasibility were established.

Use:
> “functions that these organisations may be able to test or host within defined scope”

rather than:
> “functions that they can implement in practice.”

## Reviewer B — methods

### Survives

The method now discloses:
- integrative/theory-oriented synthesis;
- structured search families;
- original/current/counterevidence positions;
- backward/forward citation chaining;
- claim-level audit;
- boundary/falsifier review;
- absence of a complete frozen historical database log.

### Remaining correction

The abstract should not call the process a “structured cross-disciplinary literature search” unless the manuscript can supply frozen database/search details.

Safer:
> “structured evidence workflow and integrative synthesis.”

Do not add database names or hit counts retrospectively.

## Reviewer C — child and family development

### Survives

The child pathway is now properly distal:

```text
premarital contact
→ usable support / safety / care access
→ caregiver and family conditions
→ nurturing care
→ child well-being
```

No direct child-development effect is claimed.

### Remaining correction

Keep this causal boundary short in the conference version. One paragraph plus Nurturing Care is sufficient; do not let child-development theory become a second paper.

## Reviewer D — safeguarding / clinical

### Survives

The manuscript correctly states:

```text
private access != safe system
```

and requires confidentiality, bounded competence, risk procedure, and protected referral.

### Remaining correction

Use “เปิดโอกาสให้ระบุความต้องการหรือข้อกังวล” for community/intermediary settings. Reserve “screening/คัดกรอง” for contexts that have appropriate tools, competence, and governance.

## Reviewer E — policy / network governance

### Survives

The receiving-system state is now more realistic:

```text
availability + connectivity + capacity + quality + governance/accountability
```

and the Thai fragmentation claim is evidence-bounded rather than asserted as a national fact.

### Remaining correction

The paper should not imply that “thin-layer” or “low additional resource” is already cost-effective. Treat it as an economic proposition to test.

## Reviewer F — conference editor

This is now the largest practical risk.

The currently formatted conference DOCX in the conversation is **23 pages**, while the NICFD template is compact, requires Thai and English abstracts of no more than 300 words each, 5–7 keywords, and APA 7 references.

The paper therefore needs a submission version that:
1. follows the template's major heading sequence;
2. compresses evidence without deleting the claim boundaries;
3. keeps only references actually needed to carry the conference argument;
4. moves technical falsifier detail into concise prose;
5. separates disclosure from promotional description of the social enterprise.

## Required submission spine

1. บทนำ / Introduction
2. วัตถุประสงค์ / Purpose
3. วิธีการศึกษา / Methodology — explicitly “การทบทวนและสังเคราะห์องค์ความรู้”
4. ผลการศึกษา / Results — explicitly “ผลการสังเคราะห์”
5. สรุปผลการศึกษา / Conclusion
6. อภิปรายผล / Discussion
   - practice architecture
   - child pathway
   - Thailand pilot
   - limitations/falsifiers
7. Acknowledgment / disclosures
8. APA 7 references

## Final content decision

Do **not** add more theory.

Do **not** add a new branded framework name.

Do **not** turn field experience into retrospective client data.

Do **not** use fertility as the first endpoint.

The next manuscript should be a **compression**, not another expansion.
