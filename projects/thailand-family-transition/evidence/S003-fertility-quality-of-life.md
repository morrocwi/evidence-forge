# S003 evidence note — Fertility quality of life among infertile women

## Citation identity

**Article:** Predictive Factors of Fertility Quality of Life among Infertile Women  
**Journal:** Nursing Science Journal of Thailand. 2025;43(2):173–184.  
**Affiliations:** Faculty of Nursing, Mahidol University; Faculty of Medicine Siriraj Hospital, Mahidol University.

## Study design

Predictive correlational study.

## Population and setting

- 123 women with primary infertility
- receiving care at the infertility unit, Siriraj Hospital
- study period: January–April 2024
- convenience sampling

## Main variables

Predictors examined:

- education level
- family income
- duration of infertility
- resilience
- infertility-related stress
- social support

Outcome:

- fertility quality of life

## Main findings

The six predictors jointly explained 62.5% of variance in fertility quality of life.

Statistically significant predictors were:

- infertility-related stress: negative association
- resilience: positive association
- social support: positive association
- education level: positive association

Family income and duration of infertility were not statistically significant predictors in the reported multivariable model.

## Implications reported by the authors

The article recommends attention to screening and support for infertility-related stress, resilience, social support, and education-related needs in infertility care, with family or close-person participation where appropriate.

## Relevance to Thailand family-transition project

**Directly supports**

- infertility is not only a biomedical issue; psychosocial and social-support dimensions are relevant to reproductive quality of life
- reproductive-health services may need mental-health and social-support components

**Does not directly support**

- that premarital education increases fertility
- that premarital intervention changes TFR
- that the identified predictors cause fertility outcomes
- population-level generalization beyond the studied clinical population
