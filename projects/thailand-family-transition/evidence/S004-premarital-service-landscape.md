# S004 evidence note — Thailand premarital service landscape

## Source identity

**Title:** Premarital ในประเทศไทยปัจจุบันและการประยุกต์เป็นระบบแทรกแซง  
**Source type:** working literature review + service-landscape synthesis  
**Status:** working synthesis reviewed  
**Verification note:** the synthesis file contains many embedded secondary citations; those citations must be checked at source level before manuscript publication.

## Service domains identified

The working review maps premarital/family-relevant functions across multiple systems:

- civil marriage registration
- public-health / preconception services
- family-development services
- faith-based premarital education
- mental-health support
- violence / social-emergency response
- parenting and child-development support

## Central synthesis

The working review's main service-level proposition is **fragmentation** rather than simple absence.

Different systems appear to have:

- different goals
- different entry times in the life course
- different content
- different access points
- different referral pathways

The review therefore treats the absence of a single, low-burden, persistent "front door" as a researchable service-design problem.

## Evidence gaps identified in the working review

The review states that current Thai evidence is insufficient to establish:

- national reach of premarital/preconception services
- standardized curriculum across systems
- long-term effects on help-seeking, referral completion, relationship safety, or parenting readiness
- an effect of premarital education on TFR
- course completion or satisfaction as valid substitutes for family outcomes

## Candidate architecture proposed for testing

The review proposes four layers as a **design proposition**, not an established intervention:

1. brief universal entry
2. optional modular learning
3. persistent digital re-entry / navigation
4. human and institutional referral

The intended function is summarized as:

```text
Recognise -> Orient -> Refer
```

## Relevance to project

**Directly supports as a working synthesis**

- the article can be organized around mapping and connecting existing systems
- "fragmentation" is a testable service-system hypothesis
- implementation research should precede strong claims of effectiveness

**Does not yet establish**

- that the mapped services are complete nationwide
- that the proposed architecture is effective
- that premarital intervention changes fertility or family outcomes
