# S007 evidence note — WHO-aligned preconception and safe/confidential care

## Source identity

This node uses current and established World Health Organization materials as external context.

Reviewed WHO materials include:

- WHO, *Reframing care and services to improve preconception health* (meeting report; 2024/2025 publication context)
- WHO, *Preconception care: Maximizing the gains for maternal and child health*
- WHO, *Health care for women subjected to intimate partner violence or sexual violence: a clinical handbook*
- WHO materials on health-system response to violence against women
- WHO, *RESPECT women: preventing violence against women*, 2nd ed. (2025)

## Preconception principle

WHO describes preconception care as biomedical, behavioural, and social health interventions provided before conception to women and couples.

This supports a broad view of preparation before pregnancy that is not limited to fertility testing alone.

Important caution: the 2024 consultation report on reframing preconception care is explicitly a meeting report, **not** a WHO guideline or formal WHO position paper.

## Safe and confidential care principle

WHO materials on violence against women emphasize that health services should be able to provide:

- privacy
- confidentiality
- survivor-centred care
- first-line support
- safety assessment
- referral to additional services

WHO health-system materials explicitly recognize that disclosure may require a safe and confidential environment and that infrastructure and patient flow should protect confidentiality.

## RESPECT framework relevance

The 2025 RESPECT Women framework includes:

- relationship skills strengthened
- empowerment of women
- services ensured
- environments made safe
- transformed attitudes, beliefs, and norms

This does **not** amount to a WHO premarital curriculum. It does, however, support integrating relationship skills, safety, services, and social norms into broader prevention thinking.

## Relevance to Thailand family-transition project

**Supports**

- the term and design principle of a **safe space / พื้นที่ปลอดภัย**
- private and confidential help pathways
- linking relationship support to professional and multisectoral referral
- treating preconception as biomedical + behavioural + social, not merely laboratory screening

**Does not support**

- calling the proposed Thailand architecture a WHO model
- claiming WHO recommends a mandatory premarital course
- claiming WHO recommends this exact four-layer architecture
