# S002 evidence note — Mahidol Model

## Source identity

**Title:** มหิดลโมเดล: รูปแบบการป้องกันและแก้ไขปัญหาการตั้งครรภ์ของวัยรุ่นในพื้นที่อย่างบูรณาการ  
**Journal:** Journal of Health Science, 2020;29(1)  
**Source type:** original research / area-based integrative model  
**Status:** primary file reviewed

## Model architecture

The Mahidol Model integrated research and practice from seven Mahidol faculties/institutes in one geographic area.

Four operating bases were used:

1. **Family**
   - family communication and relationship intervention
2. **Community**
   - local volunteers and community family-development structures
3. **School**
   - screening, support, sexuality education, counselling, and referral
4. **Hospital / health services**
   - adolescent pregnancy care, contraception, psychosocial support, follow-up

## Reported findings relevant to systems design

The family component used an RCT among families with adolescents and reported better family relationship/communication in the intervention group.

The broader model also embedded existing local personnel and institutions rather than relying on one new standalone service.

## Relevance to project

**Directly supports**

- Mahidol has precedent for area-based, cross-sector integration around a family/reproductive-health problem
- family, community, school, and health-service nodes can be designed to work as one network
- existing institutional systems can be strengthened and connected rather than replaced

**Transfer caution**

This model addressed adolescent pregnancy, not premarital intervention or low fertility. It should therefore be used as a precedent for **integration architecture**, not as direct evidence that the same intervention design will work for couples entering marriage.
