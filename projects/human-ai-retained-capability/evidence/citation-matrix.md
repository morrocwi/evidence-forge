## Core citation logic

For **P5/P8**, the manuscript should cite evidence on both sides:

- studies showing reliance, shallow reasoning, poorer retention or transfer after AI-assisted work;
- studies showing retained or delayed gains when AI is used in augmentation / explanation-oriented ways.

This supports a conditional theory:

```text
AI exposure alone
    does not determine
enduring human capability.

Mode of assistance
× learner engagement
× verification/practice
× time
× task/transfer distance
    shape what remains.
```

## Do not use citations as a vote count

More supporting sources do not make a proposition true. The matrix is used to identify:

- exact evidential bearing;
- limits;
- contradictory findings;
- boundaries;
- what remains a theory prediction.
