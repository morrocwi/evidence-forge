# Counterevidence and reduction ledger

This file exists to make disconfirmation structurally visible.

## Questions to attack

1. Does AI-assisted performance in fact predict delayed independent performance strongly enough that the P5 distinction adds little?
2. Are retention, fading assistance and transfer already fully captured by established learning-science constructs?
3. Do verification/practice/action components add explanatory value beyond ordinary time-on-task, tutoring quality or deliberate practice?
4. Can fluent dialogue itself produce comparable retention when learner engagement is controlled?
5. Is the relevant unit of analysis sometimes the coupled human–AI system rather than the unaided individual?
6. Does the preprint's "retained knowledge" bundle several separable constructs that should not be collapsed?
