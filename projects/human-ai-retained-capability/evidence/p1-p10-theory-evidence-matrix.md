## Immediate conclusion for the theory paper

The evidence base does **not** support collapsing P1–P10 into one causal claim.

The strongest current parts are:

1. **P5 — non-collapse of assisted artifact/performance and learner capability**
2. **P8 — post-assistance retention / fading / transfer as necessary evaluation targets**
3. **P7 — independence/quality of verification routes rather than answer count alone**
4. **P10 — entrusted participation has a substantial neighbouring literature, but requires careful cross-domain adaptation**

The weakest / most theory-led parts are currently:

- **P2** — direct evidence for *cross-domain route-set divergence* from pivotal term changes is missing.
- **P4** — the proposed routing-quality criterion needs direct testing.
- **P9** — institution-to-potential causal linkage remains mainly integrative/theoretical.

This asymmetry should be disclosed rather than hidden.
