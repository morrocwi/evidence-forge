# L2 — Conceptual orientation

## Purpose

Use high-quality syntheses and reference works to establish what neighbouring fields already distinguish.

## Required orientation questions

For each proposition:

1. What established construct is closest?
2. What does that construct include?
3. What does it exclude?
4. What boundary conditions are already known?
5. What original / seminal sources should be opened next?
6. Does the HARC proposition add a relation, transition or measurement distinction, or merely a new label?

## Current high-priority conceptual families

- learning retention and transfer
- self-regulated learning
- cognitive offloading / automation dependence
- epistemic cognition / vigilance
- expertise studies
- boundary crossing
- distributed / extended cognition
- capability approach
- relational trust / entrustment

## Output discipline

L2 may orient and delimit. It does not replace L3 primary-source reading when the manuscript attributes a finding or originating construct.
