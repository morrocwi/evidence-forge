# L5 — Citation / knowledge graph

## Purpose

Expand outward from anchor sources without mistaking citation relationships for evidential support.

## For every anchor source

Trace:

- references it relies on
- later works that cite it
- replications
- criticisms
- corrections
- bridge papers connecting two literature families
- sources that reach a different conclusion with similar designs
