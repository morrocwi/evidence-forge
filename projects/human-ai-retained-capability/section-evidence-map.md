## Gates

### Gate A — descriptive claim
Traceable to a reviewed source.

### Gate B — construct
Defines inclusion, exclusion, and distinction from adjacent constructs.

### Gate C — mechanism
Explains why/how; correlation is insufficient.

### Gate D — proposition
Testable and explicitly theoretical until examined.

### Gate E — boundary condition
States a condition under which a proposition changes, weakens, reverses, or fails.

### Gate F — rival explanation
Provides a plausible alternative account of the same observation.

### Gate G — falsifier
Names an observation that would materially weaken the proposition or model.

### Gate H — formalization
Symbols must map to defined constructs and observable quantities or states.
