# Theory-paper strengthening pass

## Objective

Strengthen the preprint as an **academic theoretical / conceptual article**, not convert it into an empirical research article.

## What should change in the manuscript

### 1. Add an explicit proposition discipline paragraph before P1–P10

State that the propositions differ in evidential proximity:

- some have close empirical neighbours;
- some integrate adjacent literatures;
- some are forward theoretical predictions.

This prevents all ten propositions from appearing equally established.

### 2. Add boundary conditions after each proposition or as a compact table

Minimum fields:

- proposition
- likely boundary conditions
- primary rival explanation
- observation that would weaken it

### 3. Strengthen P5 wording

Keep the current non-collapse claim, but make heterogeneity explicit:

> Assisted performance and learner capability are distinct measurement targets. Their relation should vary by assistance mode, learner engagement, delay, task and transfer distance.

Do **not** rewrite P5 as a universal negative effect of AI on learning.

### 4. Strengthen P8 wording

Make the core prediction conditional:

> Post-assistance development should depend on whether AI use preserves or creates human cognitive and epistemic work—verification, reconstruction, practice, feedback and transfer—rather than on AI exposure alone.

This accommodates evidence showing both impairment and delayed gains under different assistance modes.

### 5. Mark P2, P4 and P9 as high-value open propositions

They are not weak because they lack a citation pile. Their status should be transparent:

- P2 has evidence for prompt sensitivity but not yet direct route-set divergence.
- P4 has adjacent boundary-crossing/framing support but lacks a direct test of the paper's preservation criterion.
- P9 is an integrative institutional/capability hypothesis with limited direct causal evidence.

### 6. Add a serious rival-account paragraph

At minimum address:

- cognitive offloading / desirable difficulties
- ordinary self-regulated learning
- feedback / deliberate practice
- extended or distributed cognition
- task-performance versus learning construct validity
- institutional gatekeeping / inequality
- established entrustment theory for P10

The paper's contribution should be the **trajectory/configuration and resulting distinctions**, not ownership of each component.

### 7. Add a theory-level kill criterion near the end

Keep the current reduction/abandonment clause and make it operational:

The framework loses value if existing theories predict the same post-assistance outcomes with equal precision and the trajectory contributes no incremental boundary condition, measurement distinction or cross-stage prediction.

## Strongest core after this pass

```text
P5  assisted artifact/performance ≠ learner status
        ↓
P7  verification quality/independence matters
        ↓
P8  evaluate what remains after decisive assistance fades
        ↓
retention + unaided judgment/error detection + transfer
```

This is the paper's most defensible theoretical spine at present.

## Secondary architecture

P1–P4 explain how routes open and can be misrouted.  
P9–P10 extend the theory from individual learning into enabling institutions and socially entrusted participation.

This two-ring structure can make the manuscript clearer:

- **core theory:** P5–P8
- **upstream / downstream extensions:** P1–P4 and P9–P10
