# Conceptual core

## Working title

**Beyond Assisted Performance: Toward a Theory of Retained Human Capability in Human–AI Systems**

Title is provisional.

## Focal puzzle

AI can improve output while present. That observation alone does not establish that the human has acquired, retained, or can independently reproduce the capability.

~~~text
ASSISTED PERFORMANCE
        ≠
RETAINED HUMAN CAPABILITY
~~~

## Core theoretical problem

Current evaluations often collapse at least four states:

1. performance produced with AI present
2. performance immediately after AI withdrawal
3. performance retained after delay
4. capability transferred to a new task or context

The project asks whether these should be theorized as distinct states rather than one continuous notion of "human improvement."

## Working architecture

~~~text
AI ASSISTANCE
      ↓
ASSISTED PERFORMANCE
      ↓
SEMANTIC / COGNITIVE CHANGE ?
      ↓
WITHDRAWAL / RETURN
      ↓
RETAINED HUMAN CAPABILITY
      ↓
TRANSFER / HUMAN CONVERSION ?
~~~

Question marks are deliberate. Each link requires mechanism and evidence.

## Contribution discipline

The project must not claim novelty merely by renaming established ideas from learning science, cognitive offloading, automation, distributed cognition, or transfer theory.

Novelty must reside in a defensible relation, mechanism, configuration, measurement distinction, or falsifiable theory.
