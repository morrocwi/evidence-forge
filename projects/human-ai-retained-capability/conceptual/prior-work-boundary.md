# Prior-work boundary

The anchor preprint does not claim first discovery of problems, language, expertise, self-regulation, retention or transfer. It positions its architecture beside adjacent literatures and asks whether the arrangement adds explanatory value.

## Boundary question for this project

The literature review should not ask "who already owns this theory?" It should ask, node by node:

- what prior work SUPPORTS;
- what prior work CHALLENGES;
- what prior work EXTENDS;
- what mechanism or transition remains specific to the preprint's problem trajectory;
- whether the proposed arrangement changes observable predictions.

## Strong falsifier

If established theories already explain the P5/P8 relations and the proposed trajectory adds no useful prediction, measurement distinction or boundary condition, the paper should narrow or reduce the relevant claim rather than defend a new label.
