# Conceptual model

## Minimal model

~~~text
                 +----------------------+
                 |   AI ASSISTANCE      |
                 +----------+-----------+
                            |
                            v
                 +----------------------+
                 | ASSISTED PERFORMANCE |
                 +----------+-----------+
                            |
                  [mechanisms M1–M7]
                            |
                            v
                 +----------------------+
                 | HUMAN RETURN / TEST  |
                 +----------+-----------+
                            |
                            v
                 +----------------------+
                 | RETAINED CAPABILITY  |
                 +----------+-----------+
                            |
                            v
                 +----------------------+
                 | TRANSFER / CONVERSION|
                 +----------------------+
~~~

## Moderators / boundary candidates

- task complexity
- domain expertise
- assistance type
- interaction depth
- retrieval effort
- feedback quality
- time delay
- baseline capability
- stakes / motivation
- opportunity for practice
- model reliability
- degree of delegation

## Non-equivalences to preserve

~~~text
high assisted performance  != high retained capability
retention                  != transfer
confidence                 != capability
AI contribution            != human contribution
short-term gain            != durable learning
withdrawal test            != mechanism
~~~
