# Candidate mechanisms

Mechanisms explain why a relation should occur. They are not findings until supported.

## M1 — Active reconstruction

AI use may support retention when the human must reconstruct, explain, compare, or generate reasoning rather than merely accept output.

## M2 — Cognitive offloading

AI may improve immediate output while reducing the need to perform cognitive operations internally, potentially weakening later independent performance.

## M3 — Feedback-mediated learning

AI may improve retained capability when interaction supplies timely corrective feedback that updates the user's internal model.

## M4 — Strategy acquisition

The human may retain procedures, heuristics, or decomposition strategies observed or co-developed during AI-assisted work.

## M5 — Representation restructuring

AI interaction may alter how the human represents a problem, enabling later independent reasoning even when exact content is not remembered.

## M6 — Reliance miscalibration

High assisted success may inflate confidence in independent capability, producing a gap between perceived and retained capability.

## M7 — Automation displacement

Repeated delegation may reduce practice of a previously held skill, potentially lowering independent capability below baseline.

## Mechanism test

For every proposition, specify:

- mechanism
- observable implication
- alternative mechanism
- evidence that would distinguish them
