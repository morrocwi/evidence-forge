# Constructs

All definitions are provisional until lineage and overlap audits are complete.

## Assisted Performance

Observed task performance while AI assistance materially contributes to task completion.

**Excludes:** claims about what the human can do independently after assistance is removed.

## Retained Human Capability

Capability demonstrably available to the human after relevant AI assistance is withdrawn or reduced under a defined test condition.

**Requires:** an explicit withdrawal or reduced-assistance condition.

## Human Return

The transition from an AI-assisted state to a condition in which the human must again perform, judge, explain, or act with reduced or no AI support.

Human Return is a **test transition**, not automatically a learning mechanism.

## Human Conversion

A provisional construct for the process by which AI-assisted interaction becomes capability that the human can later deploy independently or transfer across contexts.

This construct must survive comparison with established concepts such as learning, internalization, transfer, skill acquisition, and appropriation before being treated as novel.

## Semantic / Cognitive Change

Any durable or temporary change in representations, concepts, strategies, calibration, memory, or problem-solving structure associated with AI interaction.

## Transfer

Performance of retained capability in a task, domain, or context differing from the assisted condition.

## Dependency

Reduced independent performance associated with reliance on AI support, relative to an appropriate baseline or counterfactual.

## Calibration

Alignment between confidence or self-assessment and actual capability under the relevant assisted or unassisted condition.
