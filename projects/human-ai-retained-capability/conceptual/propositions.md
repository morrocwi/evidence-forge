# Theoretical propositions

All propositions are provisional and require evidence review.

## P1 — Assisted–retained divergence

Higher AI-assisted performance does not necessarily imply proportionally higher retained human capability after assistance withdrawal.

## P2 — Engagement moderation

The relation between AI-assisted performance and retained capability is expected to be stronger when the human engages in active reconstruction, explanation, or verification than when interaction is primarily passive acceptance.

## P3 — Offloading trade-off

Under conditions of high cognitive delegation and low internal reconstruction, immediate assisted performance may increase while later independent performance remains unchanged or declines.

## P4 — Feedback pathway

AI assistance that provides interpretable corrective feedback is expected to produce greater retained capability than assistance that supplies answers without usable feedback, holding other factors constant.

## P5 — Calibration gap

High assisted success can increase perceived capability faster than retained independent capability, creating a calibration gap.

## P6 — Transfer threshold

Retained performance on the original task is insufficient evidence of Human Conversion unless capability generalizes to a defined new condition, task, or context.

## P7 — Expertise interaction

The effect of AI assistance on retained capability may differ by baseline expertise because experts and novices allocate attention, verify outputs, and integrate feedback differently.

## Discipline

These are theory statements, not findings.
