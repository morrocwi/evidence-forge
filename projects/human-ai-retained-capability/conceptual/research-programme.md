# Falsifiable research programme

## Programme question

When does AI assistance improve immediate performance without increasing durable human capability, and when does assisted interaction become independently usable capability?

## Study family A — withdrawal design

Measure:

1. baseline unaided performance
2. AI-assisted performance
3. immediate post-withdrawal performance
4. delayed post-withdrawal performance

## Study family B — transfer design

After AI-assisted learning, test performance on:

- near-transfer tasks
- far-transfer tasks
- explanation / justification tasks
- novel problem structures

## Study family C — mechanism manipulation

Randomize or compare:

- answer-only assistance
- explanation
- critique
- retrieval prompts
- self-explanation
- verification requirement
- delayed feedback

## Study family D — calibration

Collect confidence estimates for both:

- assisted condition
- unassisted condition

Compare perceived and observed capability.

## Study family E — longitudinal use

Test whether repeated AI delegation:

- builds strategy
- leaves independent capability unchanged
- or displaces previously held skill

## Minimum reporting

Every study should distinguish human-only, AI-assisted, and post-assistance states where applicable.
