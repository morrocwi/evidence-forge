# Rival explanations

## R1 — Pure tool substitution

Observed gains may require no human learning at all; AI simply performs part of the task.

## R2 — Practice effect

Post-assistance improvement may result from repeated task exposure rather than AI-specific learning.

## R3 — Selection effect

Users who benefit most may already differ in expertise, motivation, literacy, or metacognition.

## R4 — Measurement artifact

Apparent retained capability may reflect similarity between training and test tasks rather than durable or transferable learning.

## R5 — Confidence artifact

Self-reported learning may change without objective capability change.

## R6 — General tutoring effect

Any high-quality feedback source—not specifically AI—might produce the same retention effect.

## R7 — Extended cognition account

Some apparent "dependency" may be mischaracterized if the relevant unit of capability is the human–tool system rather than the unaided individual.

## Rival rule

The manuscript must explain why an individual-retention construct is useful without claiming that system-level or extended-cognition accounts are invalid.
