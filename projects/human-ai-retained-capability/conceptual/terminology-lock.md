# Terminology lock

## Use as written in the preprint

- **assisted performance**
- **retained knowledge**
- **changed human agent**
- **retention**
- **fading of decisive assistance**
- **transfer**
- **enduring human capability**
- **knowledge-like candidates**
- **human potential**

## Do not promote into new named constructs yet

### Human Return — HOLD
Not a named construct in the anchor preprint. The paper instead uses language such as the person returning to the world, changed human agent, and fading of decisive assistance.

### Human Conversion — HOLD
Not a named construct in the anchor preprint. The paper describes retained knowledge, changed capability and transfer. A separate construct requires explicit lineage and added explanatory value.

### Retained Human Capability — use cautiously
The preprint uses "retained capability" descriptively, but its named connector between cycles is **retained knowledge** in a broad sense. Do not silently elevate "Retained Human Capability" into a branded construct.

### Semantic / Cognitive Change — HOLD as a named construct
The paper discusses changes in premise structure, problem perception, epistemic regulation, skill and transfer. A unified named construct should not be added without a reason.

## Reduction rule

The preprint itself states that proposed terms should be reduced, discarded or folded into established concepts if empirical testing shows no added explanatory value. This project applies the same discipline to any vocabulary added during evidence review.
