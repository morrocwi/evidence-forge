# Evidence audit

## Audit questions

For every central claim:

- Is the source primary, synthesis, theory, policy, benchmark, or commentary?
- Is the cited population / task / model / context the same as the manuscript claim?
- Is the direction of evidence preserved?
- Is correlational evidence being upgraded to causality?
- Is a short-term performance effect being mistaken for retained capability?
- Is AI-assisted output being attributed to the human?
- Is absence of evidence being written as evidence of absence?
- Is contradictory or null evidence represented?
- Is the claim still valid under the stated boundary conditions?

## Status vocabulary

- **verified** — directly supported by reviewed source material
- **working** — plausible but source verification incomplete
- **synthesis** — cross-source integration
- **hypothesis** — inferential statement requiring testing
- **proposition** — theoretical relation
- **unsupported** — no adequate source or reasoning yet
- **rejected** — contradicted or abandoned
