# Human–AI Enduring Capability — evidence companion

## Purpose

This workspace supports the existing conceptual preprint:

**Yaoharee Lahtee, _How Can AI Expand Human Potential? From Reachable Possibilities to Enduring Human Capability_, v6.17-EN, 20 September 2026, SSRN abstract 7491779.**

It is **not a second theory paper** and it must not invent a parallel vocabulary for constructs already named in the preprint.

## Anchor question

The paper asks how AI can develop human potential **without confusing what the system helps a person do now with what later becomes part of the person's own capability**.

The evidence project therefore focuses on the paper's own distinction between:

- AI-assisted output / **assisted performance**;
- what remains with the human after time has passed;
- **fading of decisive assistance**;
- **transfer** to a new problem;
- the resulting **changed human agent**.

## Source-of-record order

1. The preprint is the source of record for this project's conceptual vocabulary and propositions.
2. `main.hub` routes programme-level authority.
3. `glosa` governs literature review, claim cards, source-first citation, independent checking and release method.
4. `toledo` is the source of record before any equation, definition or theorem is written or reused.
5. `readout_universe` governs claim-strength labels.
6. Evidence Forge stores project-specific evidence maps and synthesis; it does not replace those authorities.

## Immediate focus

The strongest evidence questions are the preprint's **P5** and **P8**:

- P5: artifact quality and what remains with the learner must be measured separately.
- P8: developed potential should be visible after assistance, with retention, fading of decisive assistance, and transfer as key measures.

## Hard guards

- Do not create "Human Return" or "Human Conversion" as constructs unless an existing programme source of record is found or the human author explicitly adopts them.
- Do not formalize new equations here before Toledo lookup.
- Do not call the literature search systematic unless it actually satisfies that design.
- Do not treat a citation in the preprint as independently verified evidence until its original source is opened and checked.
- Do not treat this preprint as peer reviewed; v6.17-EN identifies itself as a preprint.
