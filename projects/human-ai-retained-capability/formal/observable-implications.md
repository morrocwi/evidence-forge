# Observable implications

## OI1
Two systems can produce similar assisted performance but different retained capability after withdrawal.

## OI2
Two users with similar assisted performance can diverge substantially in retained capability because of engagement, expertise, delegation, or feedback use.

## OI3
If answer-only assistance induces more delegation than explanation-oriented assistance, the two conditions may have similar PA but different RHC.

## OI4
A user may report increased confidence after AI assistance even when measured RHC does not improve proportionally.

## OI5
RHC can be positive without far transfer; therefore transfer must be independently tested.

## OI6
Delayed RHC can differ from immediate post-withdrawal performance.

## Mapping requirement

Each observable implication should map to:

- proposition
- mechanism
- measurement design
- falsifier
