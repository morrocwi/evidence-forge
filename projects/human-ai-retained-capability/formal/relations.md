# Candidate formal relations

These relations are hypotheses for formal development, not established equations.

## R1 — Assisted performance

~~~text
PA = f(H0, A, X)
~~~

## R2 — Retained capability

~~~text
RHC = f(H0, E, F, D, A, τ, X)
~~~

where:

- E = active engagement / reconstruction
- F = feedback quality
- D = delegation / offloading
- τ = delay
- X = boundary variables

## R3 — Divergence

~~~text
G = PA - RHC
~~~

The theory predicts that G may be non-zero and systematically related to interaction design and user characteristics.

## R4 — Transfer

~~~text
TR = f(RHC, abstraction, context_shift, X)
~~~

## R5 — Calibration gap

~~~text
K = perceived_capability - observed_RHC
~~~

## Discipline

The project should prefer inequalities, directional predictions, and testable structural relations before imposing unjustified parametric forms.
