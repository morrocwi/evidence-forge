# Contribution statement — working

## Do not claim yet

- a general theory of human–AI intelligence
- that AI necessarily improves or degrades human capability
- that Human Conversion is a novel construct
- that the proposed mechanisms are empirically established
- that the literature search is systematic

## Candidate defensible contribution

This paper distinguishes **AI-assisted performance** from **retained human capability** and develops an integrative model explaining why the two may diverge after assistance is reduced or withdrawn. It links mechanisms from active reconstruction, feedback, cognitive offloading, calibration, and transfer to explicit boundary conditions, rival explanations, falsifiers, and post-assistance tests.

## Stronger contribution only if supported

If novelty and lineage audits show that existing theories do not already specify the configuration, the paper may contribute a formal framework for evaluating human capability across baseline, assisted, withdrawal, delayed-retention, and transfer states.
