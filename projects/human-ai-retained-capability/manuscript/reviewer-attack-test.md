# Reviewer attack test

Before submission, answer each attack with evidence and theory.

## Attack 1
"This is just learning transfer with AI examples."

Required response: show what the assisted-versus-retained architecture adds and which predictions change.

## Attack 2
"Retained Human Capability is just competence after training."

Required response: either demonstrate a needed AI-specific theoretical distinction or rename/import the established construct.

## Attack 3
"Human Conversion is unnecessary terminology."

Required response: lineage audit against internalization, appropriation, learning, and transfer. Drop the term if redundant.

## Attack 4
"Your mechanism is just cognitive offloading."

Required response: specify what offloading explains and what requires additional mechanisms.

## Attack 5
"Why privilege the unaided individual? Human–AI capability is system-level."

Required response: define the use cases where independent capability matters while acknowledging extended/distributed cognition accounts.

## Attack 6
"You have citations, not theory."

Required response: every proposition must contain a logical mechanism, boundary condition, rival, and falsifier.

## Attack 7
"This is a narrative review pretending to be systematic."

Required response: describe the method accurately as structured, theory-oriented synthesis unless systematic-review requirements are actually met.

## Attack 8
"Immediate withdrawal is not retention."

Required response: distinguish immediate return, delayed retention, and transfer empirically and conceptually.
