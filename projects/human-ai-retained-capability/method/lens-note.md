# Lens note

## Raw problem

How should the evidence behind the preprint's claim that AI-assisted output is not equivalent to enduring human capability be assembled and checked without inventing a parallel theory?

## Readout distinction

The observable joint artifact, the human's later performance, the evidence status of a claim, and the causal explanation for any change are different readouts and must not be collapsed.

## Local contrast space

At minimum distinguish:

- joint / AI-assisted artifact quality;
- learner understanding or explanation;
- retention after time;
- performance when decisive assistance fades;
- transfer to a changed problem;
- system-level human–AI capability versus human-held capability.

## Access relation

This project can directly read the preprint and public scholarly sources it acquires. It cannot infer durable human capability from polished output alone.

## Lens-out question

Under what conditions, and with what evidence, can changes observed after AI use be attributed to capability that remains with the human rather than only to the coupled human–AI system?

Status: working lens note for the evidence project; not a theorem or empirical result.
