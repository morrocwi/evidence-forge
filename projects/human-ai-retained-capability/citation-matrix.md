## Rules

1. Cite the original/seminal source when attributing an originating construct.
2. Add a current synthesis when describing the present state of a field.
3. Record contradictory or limiting evidence where it exists.
4. Never cite a review as though it were the originator of a construct it summarizes.
5. A citation cannot substitute for the logical step connecting two claims.
6. Every central proposition should be auditable back to sources and assumptions.
