# Mahidol Nursing scholarly-article schema

## Basis

Derived from the structural pattern observed in contemporary scholarly work associated with Mahidol University nursing scholarship, while keeping research genre explicit.

## Generic scholarly logic

```text
Problem / significance
  -> Current knowledge
  -> Knowledge gap
  -> Aim
  -> Transparent evidence-building approach
  -> Synthesis / findings
  -> Discussion
  -> Practice / system implications
  -> Limitations
  -> Future research
  -> Conclusion
```

## Reusable schema

1. **Introduction / Background and significance**
   - define the problem
   - magnitude and context
   - consequences
   - current knowledge
   - unresolved gap
   - purpose of the article

2. **Conceptual / evidence frame**
   - key concepts
   - definitions
   - theoretical perspectives where needed
   - relationships among concepts

3. **Approach to evidence**
   - Academic article: scope + organizing logic
   - Integrative review: search, eligibility, appraisal, synthesis
   - Systematic review: protocol/search/screening/appraisal/synthesis
   - Concept analysis: uses, attributes, cases, antecedents, consequences, empirical referents

4. **Synthesized knowledge**
   - organize by evidence domains/themes, not by author-by-author summary

5. **Discussion**
   - what the synthesis means
   - convergence with prior evidence
   - contradictions / heterogeneity
   - contextual interpretation
   - what cannot yet be concluded

6. **Implications**
   - practice
   - family/patient care
   - service/system design
   - education
   - policy only where evidence supports it

7. **Limitations**

8. **Future research / recommendations**

9. **Conclusion**

10. **Back matter**
    - funding
    - conflicts of interest
    - author contributions
    - ethics / not applicable
    - AI-use disclosure if required
    - references

## Important distinction

Do not import empirical-research sections such as population, sample, instruments, hypothesis, and statistical results into an academic/review article unless the paper actually contains original empirical research.
