# Conceptual paper working method

## Article type

The project is a **conceptual paper supported by structured evidence search and explicit equations**.

It is not being presented as:

- original empirical research;
- a systematic review;
- a narrative opinion essay.

The literature is used to reconstruct the problem, test the equations, identify counterevidence, and define boundaries.

## Working sequence

\`\`\`text
Observed phenomenon
→ international policy responses already in use
→ implicit policy equation
→ collapsed states / hidden transitions
→ Toledo non-collapse constraints
→ expanded equation
→ structured global evidence search
→ counterevidence and boundary conditions
→ observable implications
→ research agenda
→ ordinary-language translation
\`\`\`

## Questions the paper must answer

- What exactly is being measured or represented?
- Which states have been wrongly treated as identical?
- How does one state become another?
- Which mechanisms have direct evidence?
- Which relations remain only working propositions?
- When does the relationship weaken or fail?
- What simpler explanation could account for the same observation?
- What evidence would force revision of the equation?

## Evidence discipline

For major claims seek, where possible:

\[
Seminal\ source
+
Current\ synthesis
+
Critical/contradictory\ evidence
\]

Every important equation should have:

\[
support
+
counterevidence
+
boundary
+
falsifier
\]

## Writing discipline

Do not announce novelty in advance.

Do not give the framework a branded acronym.

Do not rename established access, continuity, navigation, capability or safeguarding ideas unnecessarily.

Present the equations, comparisons and evidence clearly enough that the reader can judge the intellectual contribution.
